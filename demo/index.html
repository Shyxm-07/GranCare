Run it on real phones

  1. Open this same link on each phone, signed in to your Claude account.
  2. On each phone tap who it is: Patient, Son or Neighbour. On the patient's phone tap Turn sound on.
  3. Tap Ring in 1 minute (or set a dose time below). The patient's phone rings.
  4. LATER twice, then the son is alerted. Tap his notification to mark it seen, or leave it unseen to trigger the emergency.
