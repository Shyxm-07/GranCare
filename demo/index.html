What happens

  1. 8:00 alarm rings. LATER snoozes 10 min, at most twice.
  2. 3rd alarm (8:20): Shyam (son, lives far away) gets an alert.
  3. If Shyam opens it, he has seen it and takes over. Nothing else is sent.
  4. Unread for 20 min (8:40): medical emergency. Ravi (neighbour) is called and messaged, and CarePlus Clinic nearby is notified.
